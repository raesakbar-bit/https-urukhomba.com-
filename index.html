HISTORY · ORIGIN

The First Story

THE BEGINNING OF URUKHOMBA

No one discovered Urukhomba.
No one conquered it.
No border was ever drawn around it.
It simply began as a thought —
and the thought refused to disappear.

A THOUGHT TAKES SHAPE

Urukhomba began in a quiet moment — an idea, a question, a curiosity about what a place could become if it were born from imagination instead of history.

It was not created to be real, yet slowly it began to feel real. Names appeared. Landscapes took form. Creatures, traditions, stories and memories followed.

What began as a single thought became something larger: not a country enclosed by borders, but a world that could continue growing wherever imagination allowed it to exist.

“Perhaps some places are real, even if they exist only in stories.”
